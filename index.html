About me

Steady support, from first grade to first year of college.

I earned my BA in Psychology with a minor in Education from Sonoma State University, graduating cum laude. I’m now in the MA in School Counseling program at Saint Mary’s College of California.

I’ve worked with students at almost every age: supervising literacy tutoring in elementary schools, advising and student teaching at a K–8 school, helping college students with registration, and now counseling junior high and high school students. Across all of it, I try to be a calm adult students can count on.

I’m an active member of the California Association of School Counselors.
