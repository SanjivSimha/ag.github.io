Every student, every grade, every step forward.

My educational journey began at Los Medanos College before transferring to Sonoma State University, where I graduated cum laude with a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and a minor in Education. I am currently pursuing my Master of Arts in School Counseling & PPS Credential at Saint Mary’s College of California.

I’ve worked with students at almost every age: supervising literacy tutoring in elementary schools, advising and student teaching at a K–8 school, helping college students with registration, and now counseling junior high and high school students. Across all of it, I try to be a calm adult students can count on.
